# Express Retail Platform
A Java application for managing products, inventory, shopping baskets, purchases, and express deliveries.

## Overview
The Express Retail Platform models a retail system where multiple companies sell products to customers. Customers can browse available products, add items to company-specific shopping baskets, and complete purchases.

A purchase can be split into orders and configured with a delivery option and additional services. The system also tracks inventory, calculates product prices, and supports delivery notifications.

## Main concepts
- **Products:** National or imported products, organized by category.
- **Companies:** Businesses that supply products and manage their stock and sale prices.
- **Customers:** Registered users with shopping baskets and purchase histories.
- **Shopping baskets:** Product selections and delivery preferences before checkout.
- **Purchases and orders:** Completed baskets, which may be split into multiple orders.
- **Delivery options and services:** Shipping methods and optional services that affect cost or delivery time.
- **Notifications:** Updates about upcoming and completed deliveries.
